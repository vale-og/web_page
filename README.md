# Valentina Ortúzar Garzón

Hi! I am a Ph.D. student at the Astrophysics Institute in Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile. My research focuses on dead stars, better known as **white dwarfs**. I analyse their temperature and gravity, to understand how they evolve into the very coolest objects in our galaxy, and how exoplanetary remnants can fall onto their surface and pollute them.
